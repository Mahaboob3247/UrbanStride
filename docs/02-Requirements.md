# UrbanStride Requirements Document

Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

# Product Overview

UrbanStride is a mobile-first social fitness platform designed for Indian metro cities.

Users can:

- Track activities
- Discover safe routes
- Join communities
- Participate in events
- Share progress socially
- Access emergency safety features

---

# Business Goals

## Goal 1

Build a fitness-focused social platform for India.

## Goal 2

Encourage community participation.

## Goal 3

Provide safer outdoor fitness experiences.

## Goal 4

Create a scalable SaaS platform.
