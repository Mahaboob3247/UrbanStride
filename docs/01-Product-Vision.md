# UrbanStride

## Vision

UrbanStride is a social fitness platform designed for Indian metro cities.

The platform helps users:

- Track running activities
- Track walking activities
- Track cycling activities
- Discover safe routes
- Join local fitness communities
- Participate in events
- Share activities socially
- Access emergency safety features

## Mission

To make fitness safer, more social, and more accessible across Indian cities.

## Problem Statement

Current fitness applications focus on activity tracking but lack India-specific features such as:

- Route safety scores
- Air quality insights
- Street lighting data
- Community-based running groups
- Emergency SOS functionality

UrbanStride solves these challenges by combining fitness tracking with local intelligence and community engagement.

## Target Users

- Working professionals
- Students
- Runners
- Cyclists
- Walkers

Age Group:

18-45 Years

## MVP Goals

- Authentication
- Profile Management
- GPS Tracking
- Activity History
- Route Discovery
- Community Feed
- SOS Safety Feature

## Long-Term Vision

Become India's largest fitness and outdoor activity platform.
