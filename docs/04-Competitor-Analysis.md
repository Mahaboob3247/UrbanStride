# UrbanStride Competitor Analysis

Version: 1.0

---

# Competitor 1: Strava

## Strengths

- Excellent GPS tracking
- Social activity feed
- Route discovery
- Challenges
- Large global community

## Weaknesses

- Not India-focused
- Limited safety features
- No women safety score
- No local route intelligence
- Premium features are expensive

## Opportunity

UrbanStride can focus on:

- Indian metro cities
- Safety-first experience
- Local communities
- AQI insights
- Emergency SOS
